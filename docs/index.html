    秘製家醬

A smash burger is pressed onto a hot griddle the moment you order — not pre-formed, not sitting under a lamp. That violent contact creates a crispy, lacy crust while keeping the centre juicy.

At Honbo we grind beef fresh daily, smash thin on the plancha, and stack it with American cheese, pickles, onion, and lettuce on our house potato milk bun. Simple layers, smashed with intent.

壓扁漢堡係你落單嗰刻先壓上熱板 — 唔係預製肉餅，唔會擺喺保溫燈下。高溫一壓，外層脆邊，中心仍然多汁。

Honbo 每日新鮮打牛肉，即叫即壓，配美式芝士、酸黃瓜、洋蔥同生菜，夾住自家薯仔包。層次簡單，每一口都係用心壓出嚟。

About Us

我哋嘅故事

Our Origin

Honbo 的起源

Born in 2017 on Sun Street, Wan Chai, Honbo is a home-grown Hong Kong burger joint that celebrates the simple things done right. We are a community-driven restaurant that focuses on honest, high-quality ingredients.

From our signature potato milk buns to our custom blend of double-gold medal winning beef — everything is handmade, smashed fresh, and served with pride.

2017 年喺灣仔太陽街開業，Honbo（漢堡）係土生土長嘅香港漢堡店，專注把簡單嘅嘢做好。我哋重視社區同誠實食材，唔玩花巧，只係做好一個經典漢堡。

由招牌薯仔包到雙金獎牛肉配方，所有嘢都係手製、即叫即壓，用心奉上。

「Honbo」means “hamburger” in Cantonese — local, direct, and unmistakably Hong Kong.

「Honbo」即係廣東話「漢堡」—— 一個好本地、好直接嘅名字。
